About the Analysis

This Engineering Decision Support System combines Artificial Intelligence with Multi-Criteria Decision Making to support engineering business decisions.

AHP

The Analytical Hierarchy Process determines the relative importance of the five major decision criteria:

  • Technical
  • Operational
  • Environmental & Safety
  • Financial
  • Regulatory

TOPSIS

TOPSIS ranks the available alternatives according to their closeness to the ideal solution using the criteria weights generated by AHP.

AI Assistant

The AI assistant interprets the calculated DSS results and explains the AHP weights, TOPSIS rankings and final recommendation.

Important: The system provides decision support and does not replace professional engineering, regulatory, legal or financial evaluation.

AHP

AHP determines the relative importance of the major decision criteria through pairwise comparison.

TOPSIS

Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution

TOPSIS compares the alternatives and ranks them according to their distance from the ideal and negative-ideal solutions.
