About the AHP-TOPSIS model

The model contains 7 major criteria and 19 sub-criteria.

Every decision rating must be a whole number from 1 to 9.

The seven major criteria are: Technical, Operational, Environmental & Safety, Financial, Regulatory, Commercial Feasibility, and Scale.

Initial Capital Requirement and Operating & Maintenance Burden are cost criteria. All other sub-criteria are benefit criteria.

Decision Ratings

Rate every sub-criterion from 1 to 9 for each alternative.
