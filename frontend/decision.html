About the AHP-TOPSIS model

The established AHP criterion weights are stored in the Excel decision model. You do not need to enter the expert AHP questionnaire again for each decision.

The model contains 19 TOPSIS criteria: 5 Technical, 3 Operational, 4 Environmental & Safety, 4 Financial and 3 Regulatory.

Non-financial criteria are rated from 1 to 10. Financial criteria are entered as actual Nigerian Naira amounts.
